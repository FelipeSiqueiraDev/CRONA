ALOSI - SENTAR, ABRIR O ARMÁRIO E ANDAR AGACHADO
================================================

Mesma receita do andar que deu certo: no ChatGPT, UM chat novo só para estas tarefas, UMA pasta por mensagem.
Na 1ª mensagem anexe as imagens da pasta NA ORDEM (1, 2, 3, 4) e cole o prompt daquela pasta. Nas seguintes, no mesmo
chat, anexe as imagens da próxima pasta e cole o prompt dela. Se uma sair ruim, use "gerar de novo" naquela mensagem.

  1-modelo.png          a Alosi nas 8 direções paradas (aprovada)
  2-mapa-estilo.png     o estilo e o tamanho do pixel do mapa
  3-guia.png            o guia da pose (o boneco 3D na câmera do jogo; o cabelo dele é "capacete": ignorar)
  4-andar-aprovado.png  (só no agachado) o andar normal aprovado dessas direções
  5-referencia-movimento.png (só no agachado) o andar agachado de verdade (o vídeo que o Felipe achou), de frente e
                        de costas: só para o gerador entender o movimento

Mande cada resultado no chat do Claude.


PROMPT 1 - pasta 1-sentado
--------------------------
Image 1 is the approved character (8 standing directions), image 2 the pixel art style and pixel size to match, image 3 a POSE GUIDE with the exact camera of the game: 1 row x 4, the figure SITTING on an invisible chair, in 4 directions (three-quarter front facing down-right, three-quarter front facing down-left, three-quarter back facing up-right, three-quarter back facing up-left).

Draw the character from image 1 SITTING, in the same 4 directions and order as the guide: thighs horizontal, knees bent at 90 degrees, lower legs vertical, feet flat on the floor, back straight, hands resting on the thighs. Do NOT draw the chair. Copy pose, camera angle and feet from the guide; hair, face, glasses, clothes, colors and the book on his right hip from image 1.
Same size and pixel style as image 1 (the same head size; the sitting figure is shorter only because the legs are bent). 1 row x 4, evenly spaced, the soles of the feet on the same baseline.
Pixel art: crisp square pixels, 1-pixel dark warm-brown outline, flat 3-5 tone shading, light from the top-left. NO anti-aliasing, NO blur. Background fully transparent (or solid flat pure magenta #FF00FF). No chair, no floor, no shadow, no text.


PROMPT 2 - pasta 2-abrir-armario
--------------------------------
Image 1 is the approved character (8 standing directions), image 2 the pixel art style and pixel size to match, image 3 a POSE GUIDE with the exact camera of the game: 4 rows x 3 frames. Each row is one direction (three-quarter front facing down-right, three-quarter front facing down-left, three-quarter back facing up-right, three-quarter back facing up-left); the 3 frames are: 1 standing, about to reach; 2 the right arm raised forward to a cupboard door handle at chest height; 3 pulling the door open (elbow bent, hand closer to the body).

Draw the character from image 1 doing this OPENING A CUPBOARD DOOR animation, same 4 rows x 3 frames and order as the guide. Copy pose, arm position, camera angle and feet from the guide; hair, face, glasses, clothes, colors and the book on his right hip from image 1. Do NOT draw the cupboard.
Every frame the same height and size as image 1, feet on the same baseline in each row; only the arms (and a slight lean of the body) change.
Pixel art: crisp square pixels, 1-pixel dark warm-brown outline, flat 3-5 tone shading, light from the top-left. NO anti-aliasing, NO blur. Background fully transparent (or solid flat pure magenta #FF00FF). No furniture, no floor, no shadow, no text.


PROMPT 3 - pastas 3, 4, 5 e 6 (agachado; o mesmo prompt nas 4)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Image 1 is the approved character (8 standing directions), image 2 the pixel art style and pixel size to match, image 3 a WALK GUIDE: 2 rows x 6 frames, each row one direction, drawn with the exact camera of the game. Image 4 is the APPROVED normal walk of this character in these same 2 directions. Image 5 is a MOTION REFERENCE only (a 3D sneak walk seen from the front and from behind): use it to understand the movement, never its style, character or camera.

Draw the SAME 2 directions x 6 frames as a CROUCHED SNEAKING WALK, like image 5: knees deeply bent and the feet a bit wider apart than in the normal walk, hips low, the back hunched forward (about 35 degrees), but the HEAD UP, looking forward (not at the floor); the arms loose and away from the body, elbows bent, forearms forward and the hands at knee height; short careful steps, the feet staying close to the floor, almost no up-and-down bounce. The whole figure about 75% of the standing height. Copy the direction, camera angle and the position of each leg, arm and foot from the guide; the hair, face, glasses, clothes, colors, outline and pixel size from images 1 and 4 (the same character as image 4, just crouched).
Keep the layout (2 rows x 6 columns, same order as the guide), feet on the same baseline in each row. Do NOT squash or shrink the frames to fit: make the image as wide as needed. The book is always on his right hip (do not mirror).
Pixel art: crisp square pixels, 1-pixel dark warm-brown outline, flat 3-5 tone shading, light from the top-left. NO anti-aliasing, NO blur, NO stray pixels. Background fully transparent (or solid flat pure magenta #FF00FF). No floor, no shadow, no text, no numbers.
